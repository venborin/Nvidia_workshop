Socratic Tutorial: Coupled Cluster Theory

Interactive guided questioning for PhD students in Chemistry

How this works

This tutorial uses the Socratic method: you answer each question in your own words, then receive a guiding follow-up question (not the answer). The goal is to build understanding through reasoning, not recall.

  • Type your answer in the text box
  • Click "Check my answer" — the tutor evaluates key concepts, not wording
  • If Correct, you move on with a follow-up question
  • If Partly or Not yet, you get a hint and try again
  • After finishing, you can reveal the model answer and common misconceptions

Prerequisites: Second quantization, Hartree–Fock, basic many-body perturbation theory. This covers the same material as the three course pages.
